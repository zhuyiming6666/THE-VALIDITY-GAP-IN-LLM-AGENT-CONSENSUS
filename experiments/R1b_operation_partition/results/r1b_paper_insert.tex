\paragraph{Decisive-operation partitions.}
Three annotators grouped 12 replies for each of 24 selected model--task cells,
with eight cells per benchmark and per model. Selection was stratified by
answer-grain modal mass. Replies assigned to the same operation by at least two
annotators were linked; connected components were intersected with normalised
answer classes to define the answer+operation partition. VAGUE ratings were
represented as singletons in each annotator's partition. We report cell-equal
means of $r_{\max}$, $\widehat A_U(3)$ and $r_{\max}^{2f+1}$, with 95\% percentile
intervals from 2,000 cell-bootstrap resamples. These intervals condition on the
sampled replies and consensus labels; estimates apply to the annotated cells.
Mean operation-partition ARI was $0.523$. At $f=3$, dropping majority-VAGUE
replies gave coverage $0.535$, while intersecting all three annotator partitions
with answer classes gave $0.387$.
