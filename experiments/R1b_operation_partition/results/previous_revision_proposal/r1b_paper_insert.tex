\paragraph{Direct partition annotation (R1b).}
Three annotators grouped 12 stored replies in each of 24 selected model--task cells
(eight per benchmark and eight per model), comparing the decisive operations stated
in the KEY summaries. The design samples from the 123-task common set using
answer-modal-mass strata and rotating models. It is a supplementary subset study,
not a probability-weighted estimate over all 369 model--task cells.
Two replies are linked when at least two annotators assign them to the same operation
group, and connected components define an operation partition. Since the guide permits
identical operations with different final answers, our analysis intersects these
components with normalised answer classes to obtain the answer+operation refinement
required by the coverage comparison; this intersection corrects the original scoring
implementation. Operation-only results are retained separately.
A VAGUE label is treated as a singleton in that annotator's partition; the 13 replies
with a VAGUE majority are therefore singletons in the consensus. Dropping those replies
is a sensitivity analysis that changes the reply population.

For each cell we compute the empirical modal mass $r_{\max}$, the distinct-reply
U-statistic $\widehat A_U(3)$, and the plug-in probability $r_{\max}^{h}$ of unanimity
on the designated empirical mode. At $n=3f+1$, $h=2f+1$, this is the designated-mode
certificate event. Means weight selected cells equally. Intervals are percentile
bootstrap intervals from 2,000 resamples of the selected cells (seed 20260925),
conditional on the replies and annotation aggregation; they do not propagate
within-cell sampling or label uncertainty and are not full-population intervals.
The mean operation-partition ARI is $0.523$ and pairwise co-assignment agreement is
$0.823$. Transitive closure adds 27 co-assigned pairs without direct majority support
in five cells. Intersecting all three annotator partitions with answer classes gives
coverage $0.387$ at $f=3$; using the two retained annotators' intersection after dropping
one annotator gives $0.391$--$0.420$.
The full reply pools of the selected cells give answer coverage $0.830$, while the
12-reply subset gives $0.843$; agreement of one answer-grain mean with the full common
set does not establish representativeness at the human grain. Annotator independence
is required by the guide but is not documented beyond the completed sheets.
